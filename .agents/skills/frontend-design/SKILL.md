# Calm Minimal & Anti-AI-Slop Frontend Design Skill

Approach every interface as a principal product designer crafting a calm, quiet, modern editorial utility. Every surface, spacing choice, and micro-interaction should feel effortless, unhurried, and unmistakably purposeful.

## 1. Ground Designs in Subject Matter & Real Content

- Never produce abstract marketing filler. Anchor layouts in the real product workflows: certificate serial verification, university scholarship tables, STEM Olympiad roadmaps, governorate chapter directories, interactive team constellations, and open financial ledgers.
- Let users act immediately (live search with `/` shortcut, segmented tabs, inline verification inputs, timeline scrubbers) rather than stacking static promo banners.
- Keep copy direct, sentence case, free of hype clichés, and scannable (`< 68ch` measure).

## 2. Explicit Anti-AI-Slop Ban List

Never emit these patterns:
1. **Weak White-on-Near-White Elevation**: Never place `#FFFFFF` cards onto `#FAFAFA` or `#F8F9FB` with invisible hairlines — widgets blend into the background. Always ground `#FFFFFF` widgets on `#F3F2EE` Warm Alabaster with crisp `1px solid #DFDDD6` borders.
2. **Heavy Dark Slab Fatigue**: Do not wrap every hero, header, and section card in heavy `#0A1931` / `#000000` boxes. Let daylight breathe across `#F3F2EE` and `#FFFFFF` surfaces.
3. **Uniform 2x2 / 3-Column Rounded SaaS Card Slop**: Vary layout cadence deliberately: asymmetric 12-column splits, horizontal index rows with subtle mono numbering (`01`–`04`), clean directories, and open breathing margins.
4. **Typography & Eyebrow Slop**: Never use plain `Inter` alone, never accent a single word in a heading in italic/color, never put tracked-out `ALL-CAPS · WITH · DOTS` eyebrows above headings, and never use decorative dot-pill badges or raw emoji icons.
5. **AI Gradient & Glow Blobs**: Never use purple/indigo/cyan gradients (`#7C3AED`, `#A855F7`), neon drop shadows, or glassmorphic floating pills.

## 3. Mandatory TF "Calm Alabaster & Fjord Slate" Design System

- **Page Canvas (`bg`)**: `#F3F2EE` (Calm Warm Alabaster — gentle on the eyes with unmistakable contrast behind crisp `#FFFFFF` widgets).
- **Primary Widget Surface (`surface`)**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White Paper) framed by `1px solid #DFDDD6`, `8px–12px` corners (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`), and whisper-quiet shadow `0 1px 2px rgba(22, 26, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Recessed Control Well (`well`)**: `#EAE8E1` (Soft Stone Well) with `1px solid #DCD9D0` for segmented pill-in-well controls, inputs, filter bars, `<kbd>` hints, and subtle metadata chips.
- **Ink Hierarchy**:
  - Primary Ink: `#161A22` (Calm Charcoal-Slate)
  - Secondary Ink: `#525866` (Editorial Slate)
  - Muted / Metadata Ink: `#767D8C`
- **Brand & Crafted Touch Accents**:
  - Primary Brand Accent (Fjord Slate): `#1B365D` (hover `#132847`, soft tint `#EEF2F6`)
  - Crafted Botanical Teal Accent: `#0F766E` (soft tint `#EDF7F5`, border `#BCE3DC`) for verified credentials, live indicators, and active states
  - Crafted Warm Ochre Touch: `#B45309` (soft tint `#FEF6EB`, border `#F6D9B2`) for subtle timeline markers and closing-soon badges
- **Typography**:
  - Display / Headings: `'Bricolage Grotesque', -apple-system, sans-serif` (sentence case, `-0.025em` tracking, weights 600–700)
  - UI & Body: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, sans-serif` (weights 400–600, `1.6` line-height)
  - Tabular Figures / Serials / Index Numbers: `'JetBrains Mono', monospace` (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`)

## 4. Crafted Micro-Touches & Verification Checklist

- Add quiet craftsmanship: `01`–`04` monospace index numbers on rows, `<kbd>/</kbd>` search shortcuts, segmented `#FFFFFF`-in-`#EAE8E1` tab controls, and smooth `150ms` border-color transitions (`hover:border-[#B8B4A8]`).
- Verify `hover`, `focus-visible` (`2px solid #1B365D`), `active`, loading, empty, and error states on every interactive element.
- Confirm zero `ALL-CAPS · DOT` eyebrows, zero purple/neon gradients, and clear figure-ground separation between `#FFFFFF` widgets and `#F3F2EE` canvas.
