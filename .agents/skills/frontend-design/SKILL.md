# Anti-AI-Slop Frontend Design Skill

Approach every interface as the principal design director at a boutique editorial studio hired by a client who explicitly rejected generic AI-generated templates. Every surface, type choice, and interaction must belong unmistakably to its subject matter and domain.

## 1. Ground Designs in Subject Matter & Real Content

- Never produce abstract marketing filler. Anchor layouts in the real product workflows: certificate serial verification, university scholarship tables, STEM Olympiad roadmaps, governorate chapter directories, and open financial ledgers.
- Let users interact immediately where appropriate (live filters, tabbed inspection desks, inline verification inputs, live API testers) rather than stacking static marketing banners.
- Keep copy direct, sentence case, free of hype clichés, and scannable (`< 80ch` line lengths).

## 2. Explicit Anti-AI-Slop Ban List

Never emit these tired default patterns unless explicitly requested:
1. **Weak White-on-Near-White Elevation**: Never place `#FFFFFF` cards onto a `#F8F9FB` canvas with faint `#E2E5EC` hairlines — screen contrast collapses and widgets disappear into the background.
2. **The Uniform 2x2 / 3-Column Rounded SaaS Card Grid**: Never chop every page into identical `rounded-2xl` icon-title-paragraph-link boxes stacked vertically. Vary visual structure cleanly: asymmetric 12-column splits, dark navy anchor dossiers beside crisp white paper ledgers, horizontal 12-column index rows, and tabular directories.
3. **Typography Slop**: Never use plain `Inter` alone for headings + body + labels, or accent a single word inside a heading in another color/italic.
4. **Eyebrow & Badge Clichés**: Never put tracked-out `ALL-CAPS · WITH · MIDDLE · DOTS` eyebrows above every heading, dot-pill badges (`● text`), raw emoji icons, or fake marketing metrics.
5. **AI Gradient Glows**: Never use purple/indigo/cyan gradient blobs or glassmorphism wash effects.

## 3. Mandatory TF Oxford & Stone Architectural Token System

Always create unmistakable figure-ground separation using strong tonal zoning:
- **Deep Oxford Navy Anchor (`#0A1931`)**: Full-bleed mastheads, institutional anchor panels, flagship tiles, and side dossiers (`#102447` secondary navy surface, `#1E3A6D` crisp divider, `#FFFFFF` / `#CBD5E1` foreground).
- **Warm Architectural Stone Canvas (`#ECEAE4`)**: The foundational page background behind light panels so `#FFFFFF` widgets pop decisively off the canvas.
- **Crisp Paper Widget Surface (`#FFFFFF`)**: Primary interactive cards, ledgers, certificate sheets, and form containers framed by `1px solid #CFCBC2` borders and purposeful `8px` (`rounded-lg`) corners.
- **Warm Sand Control Well (`#E2DFD7`)**: Segmented switches, secondary wells, filter bars, and tag chips (`1px solid #C8C3B8`).
- **Interactive Royal Cobalt (`#14388C` / hover `#0F2C70`) & Scholarly Brass (`#C88A14`)**: High-contrast buttons, brass focus/keystone accents, and active tab rules.
- **Ink Hierarchy on Light**: Primary `#12161F`, Secondary Slate `#4A5262`, Muted `#687082`.
- **Typography**:
  - Display / Section Headings: `'Newsreader', Georgia, serif` (sentence case, `-0.025em` tracking, weights 600–700).
  - Body / UI Controls: `'Plus Jakarta Sans', -apple-system, sans-serif` (weights 400–700).
  - Serials / IDs / Routes / Tabular Figures: `'JetBrains Mono', monospace`.

## 4. State, Contrast & Restraint Checklist

Before finishing any UI change, verify:
- Adjacent surfaces have clear contrast (`#0A1931` vs `#ECEAE4` vs `#FFFFFF` with `#CFCBC2` border).
- Every interactive control accounts for `hover`, `focus-visible`, `active`, loading, and empty/error states.
- Zero `ALL-CAPS · DOT` eyebrows, zero uniform 2x2 rounded-2xl slop grids, and zero raw emoji icons.
