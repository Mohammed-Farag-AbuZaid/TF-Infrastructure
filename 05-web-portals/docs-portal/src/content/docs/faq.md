### 1. What is TF Unions?
TF Unions is an independent non-profit initiative dedicated to helping ambitious students achieve their science-related goals. We build projects, tools, services, and communities that help students learn, grow, solve problems, and access opportunities under a shared mission.

### 2. Who is TF Unions for?
TF Unions is designed for students who have meaningful goals related to science, technology, engineering, mathematics (STEM), research, and innovation. You do **not** need to attend a STEM schoolâ€”what matters is having the ambition to learn, improve, and make progress toward your goals.

### 3. Do I need to be a STEM school student to benefit from TF Unions?
**No.** TF Unions is open to any student across Thanaweya Amma, STEM schools, IGCSE, American Diploma, Azhar, Prep schools, and Universities.

### 4. Do I need top grades to be part of TF Unions?
**No.** At TF Unions, being a standout student does not simply mean having the highest grades. We believe standout students are those who have meaningful goals, take initiative, stay curious, and actively work to improve themselves.

### 5. Why do I need a TF Unions account?
A TF Unions account gives you access to the entire ecosystem. All current and future TF Unions services use the same Unified SSO system (`auth.tfunions.com`), allowing you to access TF News, TF Guide, and TF Connect without creating separate accounts. **One account. All TF Unions services.**

### 6. Who funds TF Unions?
TF Unions is maintained by its co-founders and community supporters with a strict **$0/month SaaS overhead** philosophy. Every donation and server expense is published transparently on [tfunions.com/transparency](https://tfunions.com/transparency).

### 7. What does "TF Unions" mean?
TF originally comes from **"Altafawwoq Union"**. The shorter name "TF Unions" was adopted because it is easier to remember and reflects our mission of uniting ambitious students, projects, and opportunities under one umbrella ecosystem.
